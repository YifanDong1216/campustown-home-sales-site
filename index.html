How these prices are calculated

We exclude sales below $10,000 because the source contains implausibly low reported prices. This removes 40 sales. Sales at exactly $10,000 and all higher prices remain included; no upper price limit is applied.

Each city and state is calculated separately. The median is the middle sale price when prices are sorted; for an even number of sales, it is the average of the two middle prices. Every qualifying sale row is kept, including repeated street addresses that may represent separate units or sales. No filters are applied to dates or property type.

Source values are as reported, not independently verified against county records. These medians describe the sales in this dataset, not all homes in each city.
